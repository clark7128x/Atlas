# Atlas Design System

## Brand & Aesthetic
- **Vibe:** Enterprise, trustworthy, precise, and highly functional.
- **Goal:** Avoid the generic gray dashboard look while maintaining professional density.

## Tokens
- **Color Palette:**
  - **Primary:** Deep Indigo (`#1E293B`) - provides stability without being pure black.
  - **Accent:** Warm Amber (`#F59E0B`) - used purposefully for highlighting exact provenance text and search matches.
  - **Background:** Off-White with a warm tint (`#FAFAFA`) to reduce eye strain, deliberately avoiding clinical pure white or generic cool gray.
  - **Text:** Dark Slate (`#334155`), strictly ensuring a minimum contrast ratio of 4.5:1 on all surfaces.

## Typography
- **Primary Font:** `Geist` or `Outfit` (never system-default Inter/Arial).
- **Scale:**
  - H1: 24px, 600 weight, tight tracking.
  - Body: 14px, 400 weight, 1.5 line height for high readability in dense data contexts.

## Interactive Elements
- **Badges:** Document visibility badges (e.g., "HR Only", "Private to You") must be prominent.
- **Buttons:** Must feature real hover states (e.g., darken background by 10%) and visible focus rings (2px solid Primary offset by 2px).
